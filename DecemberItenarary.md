# Beijing Winter Family Itinerary — December 19–22, 2026

> Reviewed November 2026. The [review notes](#review-notes) at the bottom list what changed and what still needs confirming. To compare Great Wall sections, see [GreatWallSections.md](GreatWallSections.md).

These dates run from Saturday, December 19 to Tuesday, December 22, 2026, so the plan is built around **Monday, December 21**. In Beijing, almost all major museums and imperial indoor compounds close on Mondays for maintenance. Outdoor monuments and suburban heritage sites stay open.

## What is open vs. closed on Monday

- ✅ **Open on Monday:** the Great Wall (all sections, including Mutianyu), the Ming Tombs (including the Sacred Way and Dingling), Tiananmen Square, the Temple of Heaven park grounds, and the outer lake paths of the Summer Palace.
- ❌ **Closed on Monday:** the Forbidden City (Palace Museum), the main halls of the Temple of Heaven (Hall of Prayer for Good Harvests, Circular Mound Altar), the inner sites of the Summer Palace (such as the Tower of Buddhist Incense), and the National Museum of China.

Putting the Great Wall and Ming Tombs on Monday avoids every closure and puts you at those sites on a quiet weekday.

## Weather and daylight

- Sunrise is around **07:30** and sunset around **16:50**, so plan to finish outdoor sightseeing by about 16:30.
- Typical daytime highs are around 0–3 °C (32–37 °F), and night-time lows are around −7 °C (19 °F). On the Wall, the wind makes it feel much colder.
- Beijing Decembers are usually dry and sunny. Count on snow as a bonus, not a given.

## The 4-Day Winter Family Itinerary

### Day 1: Saturday, Dec 19 — The Imperial Heart

- **Morning (08:30–12:00): Tiananmen Square & the Forbidden City.** Be in the security line by about 08:15. Your guide handles tickets, but there is no skip-the-line for security. Enter at the Meridian Gate (south) and walk the central axis north. Visit the **Hall of Clocks and Watches**: its 18th-century mechanical clocks will fascinate a 10-year-old. It needs a separate add-on ticket, so book it together with the main ticket.
- **Midday (12:00–12:30): Jingshan Park.** You leave the Forbidden City through its north gate (Gate of Divine Might), directly across the road from Jingshan Park. Walk 10 minutes up the central hill for a panoramic view of the golden palace roofs, dusted with snow if you're lucky.
- **Lunch (12:45–14:00): Peking Duck.** Your private driver picks you up at the Jingshan gate and takes you to a warm indoor restaurant.
- **Afternoon (14:15–16:30): Hutongs around Houhai.** Take a covered rickshaw ride through the old hutong alleys around Houhai (ask for blankets). Stop at a local courtyard home for a hands-on activity such as dumpling making or paper cutting.

### Day 2: Sunday, Dec 20 — Imperial Parks & Lake Ice

- **Morning (08:45–11:00): Temple of Heaven.** Buy the **combined ("through") ticket**: basic park entry does not include the Hall of Prayer for Good Harvests, the Echo Wall, or the Circular Mound Altar. Arrive early, because local seniors are most active in the morning: tai chi, dancing, singing, and Chinese chess along the sheltered Long Corridor.
- **Transfer (11:00–11:45):** Drive across the city to the northwest, about 20 km. Allow 45 minutes or more on a Sunday.
- **Lunch (11:45–13:00): Mongolian Hot Pot near the Summer Palace.** Your family cooks premium meats in a bubbling broth. Eating near the Summer Palace means the long drive comes before lunch, not after.
- **Afternoon (13:15–16:30): Summer Palace.** Buy the **combined ticket**. The inner sites close around 16:00 in winter, so see them first: climb Longevity Hill to the **Tower of Buddhist Incense**, walk the **Long Corridor**, and see the **Marble Boat**. Then go down to the **Kunming Lake Ice Rink** for old-Beijing ice bicycles and sleds against the backdrop of Longevity Hill.
  - ⚠️ **Ice rink caveat:** The rink opens only once the lake ice is thick enough. In recent seasons that has been **late December**, and in 2022 it was December 31. It may well not be open on December 20. Ask your agency to check for the opening announcement a few days before.
  - **Plan B:** Spend longer on Longevity Hill and Suzhou Street, or go to an indoor ice rink (several large malls have one). The other natural rinks, such as Shichahai, depend on the same freeze, so don't count on them either.

### Day 3: Monday, Dec 21 — Great Wall & Ming Tombs (The Monday Solution)

Mutianyu is now in the morning and the Ming Tombs in the afternoon (see the [review notes](#review-notes) for why).

- **07:00** Leave in your heated private van for **Mutianyu**, about 70 km and roughly 1.5 hours away. Leaving early gets you ahead of the traffic.
- **08:30–09:00** At the visitor center, buy tickets and take the shuttle bus up to the lift area.
- **09:00–11:30 Mutianyu Great Wall.** Ride the enclosed **cable car up to Tower 14**. Walk west along the wall to **Tower 6**: about 1 km with lots of steep steps, so allow 45–75 minutes with photo stops. Finish by riding the **Alpine Toboggan down from Tower 6**.
  - The cable car (Tower 14) and the chairlift/toboggan (Tower 6) are run by **two different companies**, and their tickets are not interchangeable. Buy a **one-way cable car ticket up** and a **one-way toboggan ticket down**, not a round trip on either.
  - The toboggan closes in rain, snow, or icy conditions. If it's closed, ride the chairlift down from Tower 6 instead.
  - Check the toboggan's age and height rules for children before you go.
- **11:45–13:00 Lunch:** farm-to-table food at a heated rustic courtyard restaurant near the base of the Wall, such as Xin Shuang Quan (confirm it's open in winter). Try the fresh mountain trout and the dumplings.
- **13:00–14:00** Drive west to the **Ming Tombs**, about 50–60 km and roughly 1 hour.
- **14:00–15:15 Dingling Tomb.** Descend about 27 meters underground into the marble vaults of the Underground Palace. On a winter Monday you'll have the chambers nearly to yourselves.
- **15:30–16:15 The Sacred Way**, which is on the way back toward the city. Give your son a photo scavenger hunt among the 12 pairs of giant stone animals and 6 pairs of officials and generals, in the late-afternoon light.
- **16:15** Relax in the warm van while your driver heads back to the hotel. Allow about 1–1.5 hours, depending on Monday evening traffic.

### Day 4: Tuesday, Dec 22 — Flexible Morning & Departure

- Spend a relaxed morning picking up local winter snacks such as candied hawthorn (tanghulu), browsing indoor markets for souvenirs, or resting before your private driver takes you to the airport.
- Museums reopen on Tuesday. If your flight is in the afternoon or evening, consider the **National Museum of China** on Tiananmen Square. Entry is free, but you need to book in advance with your passport.
- Confirm which airport you fly from: **Capital (PEK)**, or **Daxing (PKX)**, which is farther south and about an hour from the center. For an international flight, aim to arrive 3 hours before departure.

## What to Ask Your Tour Agency to Lock In Right Now

This plan depends on precise ticketing, so send these notes to your guide or driver provider:

1. **Forbidden City tickets (for Sat, Dec 19):** Tickets are released 7 days in advance at 20:00 Beijing time, which is **Saturday, Dec 12 at 20:00**. That's 12:00 UTC, 07:00 in New York, and 04:00 in Los Angeles. The agency must buy the moment sales open, using your exact passport numbers, and add the **Hall of Clocks and Watches** ticket. Bring the same passports on the day.
2. **Tiananmen Square:** Ask whether you need a separate, free real-name reservation for the square on Dec 19, and if so, have the agency make it.
3. **Sunday combined tickets:** Get the combined ("through") tickets for the Temple of Heaven and the Summer Palace, not basic park entry.
4. **The Monday swap:** Confirm with the team that you are intentionally doing the Great Wall and Ming Tombs on Monday, Dec 21, to avoid the Monday closures in the city center.
5. **Mutianyu lifts:** Book a one-way cable car up (Tower 14) and a one-way toboggan down (Tower 6). Also check that the cable car's annual December maintenance day doesn't fall on Dec 21. In 2025 it was Dec 13.
6. **Ice rink status:** Have the agency check around Dec 17–18 whether the Kunming Lake rink has opened, and line up the indoor-rink Plan B.
7. **Heated minivan:** Make sure your private vehicle is a spacious, fully heated 7-seater minivan (such as a Buick GL8). That gives you a warm place to leave heavy winter gear while you walk around.
