To build the perfect itinerary for your dates (December 19–22, 2026), we must plan around Monday, December 21.
In Beijing, almost all major museums and imperial indoor compounds enforce strict closures on Mondays to allow for site maintenance. However, outdoor monuments and suburban heritage zones remain completely unaffected.

What is OPEN vs. CLOSED on Monday in Beijing

• ✅ OPEN on Monday: The Great Wall (all sections, including Mutianyu), the Ming Dynasty Tombs (including the Sacred Way and Dingling), Tiananmen Square, the public parklands of the Temple of Heaven, and the outer lake paths of the Summer Palace.
• ❌ CLOSED on Monday: The Forbidden City, the inner ceremonial halls of the Temple of Heaven (Hall of Prayer for Good Harvests), the inner exhibition halls/gardens of the Summer Palace (Tower of Buddhist Incense), and the National Museum of China.
By strategically placing your Great Wall and Ming Tombs day trip on Monday, your family avoids all closure traps while capitalizing on empty sites.

The 4-Day Winter Family Itinerary (Dec 19–22)


Day 1: Saturday, Dec 19 – The Imperial Heart

• Morning (09:00 – 12:00): Tiananmen Square & The Forbidden City. Your guide will fast-track you through security. Spend 3 hours walking the central axis, visiting the spectacular Imperial Clock Gallery to keep your 10-year-old fascinated by the 18th-century moving mechanical clocks.
• Midday (12:00 – 12:30): Cross the road to Jingshan Park. Walk 10 minutes up the central hill for a breathtaking, panoramic view of the golden palace roofs dusted in winter snow.
• Lunch (12:30 – 14:00): Have your private driver pick you up and take you to a warm indoor restaurant for authentic Peking Duck.
• Afternoon (14:15 – 16:30): Take a cozy, covered rickshaw ride through the ancient Hutong Alleyways surrounding Houhai. Stop at a local courtyard house to let your son watch a traditional cricket-fighting demonstration.

Day 2: Sunday, Dec 20 – Imperial Parks & Lake Ice

• Morning (09:30 – 11:30): Explore the Temple of Heaven. Visit the grand Hall of Prayer for Good Harvests before walking the long sheltered corridor to watch local Beijing seniors socializing and playing Chinese chess.
• Lunch (11:45 – 13:00): Enjoy a steaming, warm Mongolian Hot Pot where your family can cook premium meats in a bubbling broth.
• Afternoon (13:30 – 16:30): Head northwest to the Summer Palace. Head straight to the frozen Kunming Lake Ice Rink. Spend the afternoon renting old-Beijing ice bicycles and bumper sleds to race across the ice against the backdrop of Longevity Hill.

Day 3: Monday, Dec 21 – Great Wall & Underground Fortress (The Monday Solution)

• 07:00 AM: Depart early in your heated private van to beat any highway traffic out of the city.
• 08:30 AM – 09:15 AM: Take a brisk, fun walk along The Sacred Way. Have your son complete a photo scavenger hunt next to the 12 pairs of giant stone animals and imperial guardians.
• 09:30 AM – 11:00 AM: Tour Dingling Tomb. Descend 27 meters underground into the massive marble Underground Palace Vaults—since it's a Monday, you'll have the subterranean chambers virtually to yourselves.
• 11:00 AM – 12:00 PM: Move toward the mountains of Huairou District (~50 km).
• 12:00 PM – 01:15 PM: Farm-to-table lunch at a heated rustic courtyard near the Wall base (like Xin Shuang Quan), enjoying fresh mountain trout and dumplings.
• 01:30 PM – 04:30 PM: Mutianyu Great Wall. Take the enclosed Cable Car UP to Watchtower 14 to hike the fortress towers in the crisp winter air. Finish the day by sliding all the way down the mountain on the thrilling Alpine Toboggan bobsled from Watchtower 6.
• 04:30 PM: Relax in the pre-warmed van as your driver navigates back to your hotel.

Day 4: Tuesday, Dec 22 – Flexible Morning & Departure

• Spend a relaxed morning picking up local winter snacks, browsing the indoor markets for souvenirs, or resting up before your private driver takes you to the airport for your flight out of Beijing.
