# Beijing Winter Family Itinerary — December 19–22, 2026

> Base: **Grand Hyatt Beijing** (Oriental Plaza, Wangfujing). Reviewed November 2026.
> The [review notes](#review-notes) at the bottom list what changed and what still needs confirming. To compare Great Wall sections, see [GreatWallSections.md](GreatWallSections.md).

These dates run from Saturday, December 19 to Tuesday, December 22, 2026, so the plan is built around **Monday, December 21**. In Beijing, almost all major museums and imperial indoor compounds close on Mondays for maintenance. Outdoor monuments and suburban heritage sites stay open.

## Trip at a glance

| Day | Date | Theme | Main stops | Driving |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sat, Dec 19 | The Imperial Heart | Forbidden City · Jingshan Park · Houhai hutongs | ~15 km in short hops; the morning is on foot |
| 2 | Sun, Dec 20 | Imperial Parks & Lake Ice | Temple of Heaven · Summer Palace | ~45 km, about 2 h |
| 3 | Mon, Dec 21 | Great Wall & Ming Tombs | Mutianyu · Dingling · Sacred Way | ~180 km, about 4 h |
| 4 | Tue, Dec 22 | Flexible Morning & Departure | Wangfujing · National Museum (optional) · airport | 30–50 km to the airport |

## What is open vs. closed on Monday

- ✅ **Open on Monday:** the Great Wall (all sections, including Mutianyu), the Ming Tombs (including the Sacred Way and Dingling), Tiananmen Square, the Temple of Heaven park grounds, and the Summer Palace park and lake paths.
- ❌ **Closed on Monday:** the Forbidden City (Palace Museum), the main halls of the Temple of Heaven (Hall of Prayer for Good Harvests, Circular Mound Altar), the separately ticketed inner sites of the Summer Palace (such as the Tower of Buddhist Incense), and the National Museum of China.

Putting the Great Wall and Ming Tombs on Monday avoids every closure and puts you at those sites on a quiet weekday.

## Weather and daylight

- Sunrise is about **07:31–07:33** and sunset about **16:52–16:53**, so plan to finish outdoor sightseeing by about 16:30.
- Typical daytime highs are around 0–3 °C (32–37 °F), and night-time lows are around −7 °C (19 °F). On the Wall, the wind makes it feel much colder.
- Beijing Decembers are usually dry and sunny. Count on snow as a bonus, not a given.

## What to pack

- **Boots with good grip.** Add strap-on ice cleats for the Great Wall if it has snowed.
- **Hats, gloves, and neck warmers,** plus layers you can take off while climbing.
- **Hand warmers, water, snacks, and a thermos** of something hot.
- **Passports, every day.** Tickets are issued in your real name and checked at the entrance.
- **Lip balm and moisturizer,** because Beijing's winter air is very dry.
- **A phone power bank,** because cold drains batteries fast.

## The 4-Day Winter Family Itinerary

### Day 1: Saturday, Dec 19 — The Imperial Heart

> **At a glance:** Everything is within about 5 km of the hotel. You'll see the Forbidden City in the morning, take in the view from Jingshan Park, eat Peking duck, then explore the hutongs around Houhai.
> The morning starts **on foot**, and the van handles the short hops after that. Sunrise is **07:31** and sunset is **16:52**.

#### Schedule

| Time | Where | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **07:45** | Grand Hyatt | Meet your guide, then walk west along Chang'an Avenue (~25 min) or take metro Line 1 one stop to Tiananmen East. |
| 08:10–08:30 | 🛂 Tiananmen | Go through security. Everyone uses the same line, so arriving early is the only shortcut. Then walk north through Tiananmen Gate to the Meridian Gate. |
| 08:30–12:00 | 🏯 Forbidden City | Walk the central axis from south to north. Don't miss the **Hall of Clocks and Watches**: its 18th-century mechanical clocks are a hit with a 10-year-old. |
| 12:00–12:30 | ⛰️ Jingshan Park | Leave by the north gate (Gate of Divine Might) and cross the road. A 10-minute climb up the central hill gives a panoramic view of the golden palace roofs, dusted with snow if you're lucky. |
| 12:30–12:45 | 🚐 Drive | Your driver picks you up at the Jingshan Park gate. |
| 12:45–14:00 | 🍽️ Lunch | Peking duck at a warm indoor restaurant. |
| 14:00–14:15 | 🚐 Drive | To Houhai. |
| 14:15–16:30 | 🏮 Houhai hutongs | Take a covered rickshaw ride through the old alleys (ask for blankets). Stop at a local courtyard home for a hands-on activity such as dumpling making or paper cutting. |
| 16:30–17:00 | 🚐 Drive | About 5–6 km back to the hotel (15–25 min). |

#### Winter hours check

Every stop fits easily. The hours are from Beijing's official winter schedule (November 1 to March 31).

| Site | Winter hours | Last entry | Our time there | Slack |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Forbidden City | 08:30–16:30 (closed Mon) | 15:30 (Clock and Treasure galleries 15:40) | 08:30–12:00 | ✅ Plenty |
| Jingshan Park | 06:30–20:00 | 19:30 | 12:00–12:30 | ✅ Plenty |
| Daylight | Sunrise 07:31, sunset 16:52 | — | Outdoors until ~16:30 | ✅ Done before sunset |

- **The 08:30 start is the right call.** The Forbidden City fills up from mid-morning, and the halls are coldest and quietest early.

#### Tickets & tips

- **Forbidden City tickets** are released 7 days ahead at 20:00 Beijing time, which is **Saturday, Dec 12 at 20:00**. They're bought with your passport numbers, and you need the same passports at the gate. Add the **Hall of Clocks and Watches** ticket when you book; it's sold separately.
- **Tiananmen Square** may need its own free reservation. Ask the agency.
- **Pack light.** Bags are scanned at each security checkpoint.

#### If plans change

| If… | Then… |
|---|---|
| Forbidden City tickets for Dec 19 sell out | Try for Sunday, Dec 20 instead (those tickets are released Dec 13 at 20:00), and swap Days 1 and 2. Both days work either way round. |
| You're running late at the Forbidden City | Shorten Jingshan Park or skip it, and push lunch a little later. |
| It's too cold for the rickshaw | Shorten the ride and spend more time inside the heated courtyard home. |

### Day 2: Sunday, Dec 20 — Imperial Parks & Lake Ice

> **At a glance:** The Temple of Heaven in the morning, hot pot for lunch, and the Summer Palace in the afternoon.
> About **45 km and 2 hours of driving** in total. Sunrise is **07:32** and sunset is **16:52**.

#### Schedule

| Time | Where | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **08:25** | Grand Hyatt | 🚐 About 4 km south to the Temple of Heaven (15–20 min). |
| 08:45–09:15 | 🌳 Temple of Heaven park | Start at the Temple's covered Long Corridor, where local seniors gather in the morning for tai chi, dancing, singing, and Chinese chess. |
| 09:15–11:00 | 🏛️ Temple of Heaven halls | See the Hall of Prayer for Good Harvests, the Echo Wall, and the Circular Mound Altar. |
| 11:00–11:45 | 🚐 Drive | About 20 km across the city to the northwest. Allow 45 minutes or more on a Sunday. |
| 11:45–13:00 | 🍽️ Lunch | Mongolian hot pot near the Summer Palace, where your family cooks premium meats in a bubbling broth. |
| 13:15–15:00 | 🏯 Summer Palace inner sites | Climb Longevity Hill to the **Tower of Buddhist Incense**, walk the **Long Corridor**, and see the **Marble Boat**. |
| 15:00–16:30 | ⛸️ Kunming Lake ice rink | If it's open, ride old-Beijing ice bicycles and sleds with Longevity Hill behind you. If it isn't, see "If plans change" below. |
| 16:30–17:30 | 🚐 Drive | About 20 km back to the hotel (45–60 min). |

#### Winter hours check

Every stop fits. The ice rink is the only uncertainty.

| Site | Winter hours | Last entry | Our time there | Slack |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Temple of Heaven park | 06:30–22:00 | 21:00 | 08:45–11:00 | ✅ Plenty |

- **Correction:** The first review said the Summer Palace's inner sites close around 16:00. The official winter hours are last entry 16:30 and closing 17:00, so there's more slack than the first review suggested. Seeing them first is still right, because the rink may not be open.

#### Tickets & tips

- **Buy the combined ("through") ticket at both sites.** Basic entry covers only the park grounds, not the halls or the Tower of Buddhist Incense. The Summer Palace's off-season combined ticket is about CNY 50.
- **The ice rink** opens only once the lake ice is thick enough. In recent seasons that has been **late December**; in 2022 it was December 31. Ask the agency to check for the opening announcement a few days before.

#### If plans change

| If… | Then… |
|---|---|
| The ice rink isn't open | Spend longer on Longevity Hill and Suzhou Street, or go to an indoor ice rink (several large malls have one). Other natural rinks, such as Shichahai, depend on the same freeze. |
| Sunday traffic is heavy | Shorten the Temple of Heaven, and make sure you reach the Summer Palace inner sites before their 16:30 last entry. |
| Someone is too cold | Hot pot is the warm-up, and the van is your warm base between stops. |

### Day 3: Monday, Dec 21 — Great Wall & Ming Tombs (The Monday Solution)

> **At a glance:** Mutianyu in the morning, lunch near the Wall, and the Ming Tombs in the afternoon. You'll be back at the hotel around 17:30–18:00.
> About **180 km and 4 hours of driving** in total. Sunrise is **07:32** and sunset is **16:52**.

#### Schedule

| Time | Where | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **07:00** | Grand Hyatt | Leave in the heated van. It's still dark until about 07:30, so ask the hotel for an early breakfast or a breakfast box. |
| 07:00–08:30 | 🚐 Drive | About 75 km (~1.5 h) northeast to Mutianyu. |
| 08:30–09:00 | Mutianyu visitor center | Buy tickets and use the restrooms. Walk through the shopping street to the shuttle bus, which takes about 10 minutes up to the lift area. |
| 09:00–09:15 | 🚡 Cable car | Ride the enclosed **cable car up to Tower 14**. |
| 09:15–10:30 | 🧱 On the Wall | Walk from **Tower 14 to Tower 6**: about 1 km, with some steep stairs. Allow 45–75 minutes with photo stops. |
| 10:30–11:15 | 🛷 Toboggan | Ride the **toboggan down from Tower 6**, then take the shuttle bus back to the visitor center. |
| 11:15–11:45 | Buffer | Spare time for queues, warming up, or the shopping street. |
| 11:45–13:00 | 🍽️ Lunch | A heated farmhouse restaurant near the Wall, such as Xin Shuang Quan (confirm it's open in winter). Try the fresh mountain trout and the dumplings. |
| 13:00–14:00 | 🚐 Drive | About 50–60 km (~1 h) west to the Ming Tombs. |
| 14:00–15:15 | Dingling Tomb | Go about 27 m underground into the marble vaults of the Underground Palace. On a winter Monday you may have it nearly to yourselves. |
| 15:15–15:30 | 🚐 Drive | About 7 km south to the Sacred Way. |
| 15:30–16:20 | The Sacred Way | Give your son a photo scavenger hunt among the 12 pairs of stone animals and 6 pairs of officials and generals, in low golden light. If the driver can meet you at the far end, you only walk the avenue one way (about 1 km). |
| 16:20–17:45 | 🚐 Drive | About 45–50 km (1–1.5 h) back to the hotel. Monday evening traffic builds after 17:00. |

#### Winter hours check

Every stop has at least an hour to spare before its last entry, and all outdoor sightseeing ends before sunset. These are winter hours (mid-November to mid-March) published by ticketing and travel sites. Ask the agency to confirm them for 2026.

| Site | Winter hours | Last entry | Our time there | Slack |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mutianyu cable car | 08:00–17:00 | Sales stop ~1 h before closing | 09:00 | ✅ Plenty |
| Mutianyu chairlift & toboggan | 08:30–16:30 | Probably ~15:30 | ~10:30 | ✅ ~5 h |
| Dingling (Ming Tombs) | 08:30–17:00 | 16:30 | 14:00–15:15 | ✅ 75 min |
| Sacred Way | 08:30–17:00 | 16:30 | 15:30–16:20 | ✅ 60 min |
| Daylight | Sunrise 07:32, sunset 16:52 | Usable light until ~17:20 | Outdoors 09:00–16:20 | ✅ Done before sunset |

- **Mutianyu has to come first.** The toboggan closes at 16:30 in winter, and ticket sales probably stop around 15:30. With the original afternoon plan (Mutianyu 13:30–16:30), you would have reached Tower 6 right around that cutoff.
- **The downside is the cold.** 09:00–10:30 on the Wall is the coldest part of the day: well below freezing, and colder with wind chill. Dress for it, and use the van as a warm base between stops.

#### Tickets & tips

- The **cable car** (Tower 14) and the **chairlift/toboggan** (Tower 6) are run by **two different companies**, and their tickets aren't interchangeable.
- Buy a **one-way cable car ticket up** and a **one-way toboggan ticket down**. Don't buy a round trip on either.
- The toboggan is a one-person sled on a metal track, with a hand lever to control the speed. Check the age and height rules for your son before you go.
- Some ticket listings say riders aged 60 and over aren't allowed on the toboggan. If that applies to anyone in your group, they can take the chairlift down from Tower 6 instead.

#### If plans change

| If… | Then… |
|---|---|
| The toboggan is closed (snow, ice, rain, or wind) | Take the chairlift down from Tower 6. It's the same company, and you can buy the ticket there. If that's closed too, the stairs down take roughly 30 minutes. |
| The cable car is closed (for example, annual maintenance) | Take the chairlift up to Tower 6, walk a shorter stretch, and come back to Tower 6 for the toboggan or chairlift down. |
| Mutianyu is closed or the weather is bad | Go to **Badaling** (cable cars) or **Juyongguan** (no lift) instead. Both are near the Ming Tombs, so the afternoon stays the same. See [GreatWallSections.md](GreatWallSections.md). |
| You're running late | Make sure you reach **Dingling before its 16:30 last entry**, and shorten the Sacred Way. |
| Someone is too cold | Head back to the warm van. Dingling's underground chambers are also out of the wind. |

## What to Ask Your Tour Agency to Lock In Right Now

This plan depends on precise ticketing, so send these notes to your guide or driver provider:

1. **Forbidden City tickets (for Sat, Dec 19):** Tickets are released 7 days in advance at 20:00 Beijing time, which is **Saturday, Dec 12 at 20:00** (12:00 UTC, 07:00 in New York, 04:00 in Los Angeles). The agency must buy the moment sales open, using your exact passport numbers, and add the **Hall of Clocks and Watches** ticket. Bring the same passports on the day. **Backup:** if Dec 19 sells out, try Sun, Dec 20 (released Dec 13 at 20:00) and swap Days 1 and 2.
2. **Tiananmen Square:** Ask whether you need a separate, free real-name reservation for the square on Dec 19. If so, have the agency make it.
3. **Sunday combined tickets:** Get the combined ("through") tickets for the Temple of Heaven and the Summer Palace, not basic park entry.
4. **The Monday swap:** Confirm with the team that you're intentionally doing the Great Wall and Ming Tombs on Monday, Dec 21, to avoid the Monday closures in the city center.
5. **Mutianyu lifts:** Book a one-way cable car up (Tower 14) and a one-way toboggan down (Tower 6). Also check that the cable car's annual December maintenance day doesn't fall on Dec 21; in 2025 it was Dec 13.
6. **Ice rink status:** Have the agency check around Dec 17–18 whether the Kunming Lake rink has opened, and line up the indoor-rink Plan B.
7. **Pickups at the Grand Hyatt:** Day 1 at 07:45 (the guide meets you to walk), Day 2 at 08:25, and Day 3 at 07:00. Set Day 4's time from your flight.
8. **National Museum (optional, Day 4):** Book with your passports, up to 7 days ahead.
9. **Heated minivan:** Make sure your private vehicle is a spacious, fully heated 7-seater minivan (such as a Buick GL8). That gives you a warm place to leave heavy winter gear while you walk around.
