# Tour Operators & Rough Prices

These are options for running the [December itinerary](DecemberItenarary.md) with a private car and guide, for **2 adults and 1 child (age 10)**. The Grand Hyatt's quotes of **RMB 4,000 per day for all three of you** are the benchmark (details below).

> **Rough prices only.** They come from operators' own sites and booking platforms listing 2025–26 rates. Many listings don't say exactly what's included, so get a written quote for your exact dates. US-dollar prices are converted at about **RMB 6.7 per USD**, the rate in September 2026. Every figure is a **total for all three of you, per day**, unless marked otherwise.
>
> Operators are listed because they appear often in reviews and on booking sites. This list is not an endorsement, and licenses haven't been checked.

**Before booking the Great Wall tour, confirm:**

1. **A 07:00 pickup, not 09:00.** Leaving at 09:00 puts you at the Wall around 10:30, and at the Ming Tombs too late to be comfortable before the toboggan's likely ~15:30 last ticket and Dingling's 16:30 last entry.
2. Whether the **Mutianyu shuttle bus** is included.
3. **No overtime charge** for a day of about 07:00–17:45 and 180 km.
4. A **licensed English-speaking guide**, **no shopping stops**, and a **heated 7-seater**.
5. That they'll buy the cable car up and toboggan down tickets for you (you pay the ~¥420), and check that **Dec 20** isn't the cable car's annual maintenance day.

**Suggested way to use the hotel:**

- **Sun (Great Wall):** Book the hotel's tour, with the 07:00 start.
- **Sat (Forbidden City):** Book a guide only. It's a walk from the hotel, for about RMB 2,000 all in.
- **Tue (Temple of Heaven + Summer Palace):** Ask the hotel for a price on just these two, and compare it with an outside operator (about RMB 2,400–3,600).
- If you'd rather use the hotel's one-day city tour, do it on **Saturday or Tuesday**, and use the freed-up day for rest, the National Museum, or the ice rink.

**Compared with our plan:**

| Site | Hotel's one-day tour | Our plan (Sat + Tue) |
|---|---|---|
| Temple of Heaven | ~1 h, mid-morning | ~2¼ h, early, while local seniors are out doing tai chi |
| Tiananmen Square | ~20 min | Passed through on the walk in |
| Forbidden City | ~2.5 h, entering ~11:00 when it's busiest | ~3.5 h from the 08:30 opening, plus the Jingshan view |
| Summer Palace | ~1¼ h, arriving 15:15 as the light fades | ~3¼ h, including the ice rink if it's open |
| Driving | ~2 h | Less, spread over two days |
| **Total sightseeing** | **~5 h** | **~9 h over two days** |

**What gets squeezed out of the one-day version:**

- The **Hall of Clocks and Watches**, which needs 30–45 minutes, or you rush the main halls.
- **Climbing Longevity Hill** and the **Kunming Lake ice rink**.
- The **Jingshan Park** view over the palace roofs.
- **Slack.** A slow security queue or a long lunch cuts into the Summer Palace, and the cold late afternoon is the worst time to be there with a 10-year-old.

**If we take the hotel's one-day tour anyway:**

1. **Ask for an 08:15 start,** so we reach the Forbidden City at its 08:30 opening, before the crowds.
2. **Drop one site,** ideally the Summer Palace, which is the farthest and gets the worst light. Three sites in a day is comfortable; four is a sprint.
3. **Ask for their actual timetable,** with minutes at each stop, and whether tickets (including the Clock Gallery) are included.

**Recommendation:** Split it as our plan does. Do the Forbidden City on Saturday with a guide, on foot from the hotel, and the Temple of Heaven and Summer Palace on Tuesday. Use the hotel for the Great Wall day on Sunday, with a 07:00 start.

## The short answer

- **Day 2 (Sun): Great Wall + Ming Tombs.** The hotel's RMB 4,000 includes entry tickets but not lifts or lunch, so it comes to about RMB 4,800 all in. That's acceptable but at the top of the market. All-inclusive private tours start at about RMB 2,400, and most cost RMB 2,400–4,800. A car and guide you arrange yourself works out at about RMB 3,300–4,000 once tickets, lunch, tips, and overtime are added.
- **Days 1 and 4 (city days):** RMB 4,000 is on the high side. Day 1 is mostly on foot and barely needs a car, so a guide alone, plus a couple of taxi rides, covers it for about RMB 2,000 in total.
- **Day 3 (Mon):** Everything is close to the hotel, so you can skip the tour. Use taxis or a van for short hops, or book a guide only.
- **Day 0 (Fri) and Day 5 (Wed):** Book airport transfers only. For KE2052 that's a pickup at 08:00 to PEK Terminal 2.
- **Ask the hotel what its RMB 4,000 includes:** tickets, lunch, tolls and parking, and how many hours and kilometers. Then compare quotes like for like.

## Questions to ask every operator, including the hotel

1. **What exactly is included?** Entry tickets (including Mutianyu's cable car, toboggan and shuttle, and the Clock Gallery), lunch, tolls and parking, and water.
2. **How many hours and kilometers are included?** The Great Wall day (Sun) runs about 07:00–17:45 and 180 km.
3. **Is it a licensed English-speaking guide,** and can you keep the same guide for every day?
4. **Will they put "no shopping stops" in writing?** That means no detours to jade, silk or tea shops.
5. **What vehicle?** Ask for a heated 7-seater such as a Buick GL8, and a booster seat if you want one for your son.
6. **Will they buy your Forbidden City tickets** the moment they go on sale (Dec 12 at 20:00 Beijing time), using your passports? Will they make the Tiananmen Square reservation?
7. **What's the weather and cancellation policy?** For example, if the toboggan or cable car is closed, or it snows.
8. **How do you pay** (international card, WeChat Pay or Alipay), and what are the tipping expectations? About RMB 100–200 per day in total is typical.

## A cost-saving mix to consider

| Day | Suggestion | Rough cost for 3 (RMB) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Guide only. Walk to the Forbidden City, then take taxis to lunch and Beihai Park. | ~2,000 including tickets and lunch |
| 2 (Sun) | All-inclusive private tour, or the hotel if its RMB 4,000 includes tickets and lunch | ~2,400–4,000 |
| 3 (Mon) | No tour needed: taxis or a van for short hops, the Lama Temple, a rickshaw and dumpling lunch, and acrobatics | ~1,500–2,500 including the rickshaw, lunch, and show tickets |
| 4 (Tue) | Temple of Heaven + Summer Palace: all-inclusive private tour, or car + guide | ~2,400–3,600 |
| 0 and 5 | Airport transfers only (Fri evening in, Wed 08:00 out) | Ask for transfer-only prices |

That comes to roughly **RMB 8,300–12,100 for Days 1–4**, compared with about **RMB 16,000** for four days with the hotel. The hotel is simpler, and if anything goes wrong you deal with one company you already know.
